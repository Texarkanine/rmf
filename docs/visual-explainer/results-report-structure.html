5 — Copy and tone

What the report is supposed to say

The PRD is specific about voice. The landing cards already demonstrate the punch/fix pair. The current scoring prompt asks for marketer-actionable findings, not jokes — so the roast layer still has to be designed on top of (or instead of) that JSON.

Do this

Kill Tony of the funnel

  • Light roast of the gaps, with receipts
  • Specific and slightly mean about the mismatch, buried price, leftover generic homepage
  • Short jabs on the canvas, not a separate essay
  • Spend as ammunition: “you’re lighting $X/mo and the homepage still ignores the ad”
  • Consultant-useful: each jab should imply a fix
  • One-line roast per score bucket

Do not do this

Not a reply-guy, not a brand dunk

  • Do not punch the brand’s customers
  • Do not dunk on the company as people
  • Do not show Ad Library UI chrome
  • Do not write a 20-page CRO memo
  • Do not invent pages, CTAs, or errors that are not in the evidence
  • Grok does not watch video as a movie — frames + copy only
Copy shape already on the landing page. Each roast card is two beats: punch (“Great lighting. Still no idea what you sell.”) then fix (“Show the product and the benefit in the first second.”). The sample punch ticket repeats that pair at report scale. That is the closest existing plan for what the sentences look like, even though the PRD never names “punchline / fix” as fields.

Marketingskills is the rubric brain, not the interview: invert ad-creative for creative, use cro for message match / on-site / checkout. copywriting + image skill draft the replacement LP mock.
