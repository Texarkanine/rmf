1 — Verdict

What “match the image” actually means

The original mock is vintage theater / comedy club, but it is restrained. Dark sections are almost empty: black, a spotlight, side curtains, type, one gold bar. Light sections are parchment with a thin gold edge and a few ticket props. The live build kept the props and then filled the empty space.

Three kinds of difference show up again and again:

  1. Extras the mock never had — brick texture, curtain swag, footlights, the gold logo-mark above headlines, ornate corner frames, extra taglines.
  2. Missing or swapped props — the landing-page ticket icon, 5 chili peppers, gold lines from roast cards to the film, a footer ornament, the original hero line.
  3. Layout that is close but heavier — cream wrapper around the CTA, punch-and-fix copy on the tape cards, brown ornate frames instead of a gold hairline.

3 — Atmosphere

The room is wrong before any section is

Original

  • Solid black stage. One gold spotlight from the top center.
  • Red velvet curtains only on the left and right of the hero, and again at the finale corners.
  • Parchment sections with a thin gold border. No brick. No footlights. No valance.
  • Tall condensed playbill type. Body / lede reads as serif or slab, not a UI sans.

Live

  • Brick wall (comedy-club-brick.png) on every .stage section, plus grain and spotlight dust.
  • StageDressing always draws side curtains. Hero and finale also get swag + footlights. The tape section gets curtains too.
  • Parchment sections sit in a dark-brown box with gold ornate corners.
  • Display type is League Gothic (condensed sans). Lede is DM Sans. Libre Baskerville is loaded and almost unused on the landing page.

Must-fix Drop the brick on the landing stages. The mock is a black void, not a club interior. Keep the radial gold spotlight and the light grain if you want texture.

Must-fix Curtains only on the hero and the finale. Remove them from “Let’s review the tape.” That section is a black film-review board, not another stage.

Should-fix Swag valance and footlight bar are extra. They are good kit pieces, but they are not in the original. Strip them from the landing page if the goal is to match the image. Keep them on scan / results if those pages need more scenery.

Should-fix Use Libre Baskerville (already loaded) for the hero lede and parchment subheads. Leave Bebas Neue on labels. League Gothic can stay as the condensed poster face, or swap to a heavier wood-type if the mock still feels more serif than gothic after the other fixes.

11 — Fix plan

What to change, in order

Work in LandingPage.tsx and styles.css first. Only generate a new image for the landing-page icon. Do not rebuild the kit.

Pass 1 is subtractive. Remove brick, extra curtains, logo-marks, and extra lines. The page will look more like the mock before you add anything.

Pass 1 — subtract and restore copy

  1. Black stages, not brick. On .stage, drop comedy-club-brick.png. Keep the gold radial spotlight. Optionally keep grain at low opacity. web/src/styles.css · .stage, .how-section
  2. Curtains only where the mock has them. Hero and finale keep side curtains. Tape section: no StageDressing curtains. Landing hero/finale: turn off swag and footlights if matching the image. web/src/components/LandingPage.tsx · StageDressing props
  3. Delete the extra logos and lines. Remove .hero-mark from hero and finale. Hero tagline → “Roast the funnel. Not the marketer.” Delete “Good news: it’s fixable.” LandingPage.tsx
  4. Form. Cream input, gold button, no cream padded wrapper. Default / placeholder https://yourstore.com. LandingPage.tsx · .roast-form in styles.css
  5. Tape cards. Title + roast only. No fix line on those three cards. LandingPage.tsx · RoastCard
  6. Chili scale to 5. Map 1–5. Sample scores should leave empty peppers showing. LandingPage.tsx · HEAT + peppers map

Pass 2 — replace the missing props

  1. Landing-page ticket icon. Same engraved gold-on-transparent treatment as icon-ads.png. Wire it through kit.iconLanding. web/public/kit/icons/icon-landing.png · kit.tsx · TICKETS
  2. Parchment frames. Thin gold border. No ornate corners, no section star on “Every click is material.” styles.css · .ornate-frame · LandingPage.tsx FrameCorners / KitStar
  3. Marquee heading flourishes. Same Flourish wrap used on the tape heading. Marquee sits on black, not brick. LandingPage.tsx · how-section
  4. Gold connectors from roast cards to the red film marks. Footer: audio-jack.png on the right, quieter nav color. styles.css · .gold-arrow / new connectors · .site-footer
  5. Type. Lede and parchment subheads in Libre Baskerville. Keep League Gothic on the giant lines unless a heavier condensed face is chosen later. styles.css · .lede, .subhead
After pass 1, screenshot the landing page next to the original. If it still feels “clubbier” than the mock, the leftovers are swag, footlights, and the ornate frames. Those are pass 2. Do not regenerate curtains, tickets, or marquee icons unless a specific asset is wrong — most of the kit is already the original’s vocabulary.
